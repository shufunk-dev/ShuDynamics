# 🎛️ Harmonic Record Press

The **Harmonic Record Press** is an advanced acoustic manufacturing unit introduced in **ShuDynamics 2.1: The Harmonic Convergence**. It uses precise thermal needle lathing and harmonic resonance frequencies to etch high-fidelity audio tracks onto vinyl discs using thematic material catalysts.

## ⚡ How It Works

1. **Power**: Connect the Harmonic Record Press to an energy grid via cables, or insert a charged Battery Pack / [Infinite Dimensional Matrix](/convergence/boss-and-relics#the-infinite-dimensional-matrix) into the bottom-right battery discharge slot.
2. **Blank Vinyl**: Insert **Blank Vinyl Discs** (`enchantedwood:blank_vinyl_disc`) into the upper left slot (Slot 0).
3. **Song Catalyst**: Place the required harmonic catalyst item into the upper right slot (Slot 1).
4. **Press Cycle**: When powered, the lathe needle engages for 5 seconds (100 ticks), consuming 5,000 FE and producing the corresponding custom music disc in the output slot (Slot 2).

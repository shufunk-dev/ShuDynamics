# 📦 Digital Storage Network, Wireless & Enchanted Chests

ShuDynamics provides high-capacity modular **Enchanted Chests** and a digital **Storage Network** to organize, search, and access thousands of items wirelessly from anywhere in the multiverse!

---

## 🧰 Modular Enchanted Chests & Tier Upgrades

Enchanted Chests can be placed as a base chest and **directly upgraded in-world** without breaking the chest or spilling your items!

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(26,28,43,0.9), rgba(18,20,32,0.95)); border: 1px solid rgba(139,92,246,0.35); border-radius: 14px; padding: 20px; margin: 20px 0;">
  <h3 style="margin: 0 0 10px 0; color: #c4b5fd; font-size: 17px;">✨ The In-World Right-Click Upgrade Mechanic</h3>
  <ol style="margin: 0; padding-left: 20px; color: #d1d5db; font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.8;">
    <li>Place down your <strong>Enchanted Chest</strong>.</li>
    <li>Have <strong>1x Enchanted Redstone</strong> in your inventory (catalyst fuel).</li>
    <li>Hold the target <strong>Enchanted Gear</strong> (e.g. <em>Enchanted Copper, Bronze, Iron, Gold, Diamond, or Netherite Gear</em>) in your main hand.</li>
    <li><strong>Right-click</strong> the placed chest!</li>
  </ol>
  <p style="margin: 10px 0 0 0; font-size: 13px; color: #38bdf8;">
    🎉 The chest will instantly transform to that tier, updating its custom trim and expanding its slot capacity with zero item loss!
  </p>
</div>

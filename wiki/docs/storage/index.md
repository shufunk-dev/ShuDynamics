### 🖥️ Smart Interface: Pagination, 1-Click Sort & Jade / HUD Integration

Every Enchanted Chest is designed for frictionless large-scale inventory management:
- **54-Slot Clean Page View**: Regardless of whether your chest holds 54, 72, 90, 117, 135, or 162 items, the GUI renders a clean $9\times6$ viewport with **`[ < ]`** and **`[ > ]`** pagination controls to seamlessly flip between storage banks.
- **1-Click Auto-Sort**: Click the **`[ 🔃 ]`** button in the top-right corner to automatically organize, merge partial stacks, and alphabetize all items across all pages instantly.
- **Dynamic Tier Titles**: The container screen header dynamically reflects the exact tier name of the opened block (e.g. *"Netherite Enchanted Chest"*, *"Gold Enchanted Chest"*, *"Copper Enchanted Chest"*).
- **Jade / Waila HUD Support**: Looking at any placed Enchanted Chest with Jade, Waila, or TheOneProbe displays the accurate tier name, custom block model, and current item capacity directly on your in-game HUD.

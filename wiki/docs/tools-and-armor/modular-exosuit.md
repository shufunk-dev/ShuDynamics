# 🦺 Modular Power Exosuit & Upgrades Suite

Introduced in **ShuDynamics 2.0** and supercharged in **v2.1**, the **Modular Power Exosuit** is an advanced cybernetic armor system designed for extreme exploration, high-mobility combat, and survival within hazardous dimensions.

## 🛡️ Exosuit Chassis Pieces & Emergency Chassis Lock

Crafted using **Titanium Ingots**, **Steel Plates**, and **Insulated Cables**:

* **Modular Power Helmet** (`enchantedwood:modular_power_helmet`)
* **Modular Power Chestplate** (`enchantedwood:modular_power_chestplate`)
* **Modular Power Leggings** (`enchantedwood:modular_power_leggings`)
* **Modular Power Boots** (`enchantedwood:modular_power_boots`)

Each piece features an internal energy buffer, accepts battery cells or [Infinite Dimensional Matrices](/convergence/boss-and-relics#the-infinite-dimensional-matrix), accommodates coprocessor logic chips, and houses **two dedicated hot-swappable upgrade module sockets**.

### 🔒 Unbreakable: Emergency Chassis Lock Failsafe
In **v2.1**, all four modular power exosuit pieces are protected by an automatic **Emergency Chassis Lock**:
* If incoming damage would reduce the piece's durability to 0, the emergency circuit intervenes, nullifies the destructive impact, and permanently **locks the chassis at 1 HP**.
* Your customized armor pieces, expensive installed modules, and computer chips **can never break or vanish from durability loss**!

## 🖥️ The In-Game Access Panel (`V` Key)

Equipping any Modular Power Exosuit piece allows the player to open the **Exosuit Access Panel** by pressing the **`V`** key at any time (or by right-clicking a Powered Anvil):

* **Armor Tabs**: Switch between **Tab 0 (Helmet)**, **Tab 1 (Chestplate)**, **Tab 2 (Leggings)**, and **Tab 3 (Boots)**.
* **Slot 0 (Battery Slot)**: Houses portable battery units (Copper, Aluminum, Steel, or Tungsten Battery Packs) or the **Infinite Dimensional Matrix** to recharge the piece's internal power supply.
* **Slot 1 (Logic Chip Slot)**: Accepts **Basic**, **Advanced**, or **Quantum Computer Chips** to accelerate auto-repair frequency, overclock repair delay, and enhance module efficiency.
* **Slot 2 & 3 (Module Sockets A & B)**: Install any piece-compatible upgrade modules.
* **Interactive Tooltips**: Hovering over empty slots displays detailed guidelines describing compatible modules and energy specs.

## ⚡ The 9 Hot-Swappable Upgrade Modules

### 1. Night Vision Module (`enchantedwood:night_vision_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Helmet (Tab 0).
* **Function**: Automatic tactical HUD illumination. Powers on automatically when ambient light levels drop ($\le 6$, drains 2 FE/t) and powers down in daylight ($\ge 9$) to conserve battery charge.

### 2. Hydrogen Thruster Module (`enchantedwood:hydrogen_thruster_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Chestplate (Tab 1).
* **Function**: High-thrust rocket propulsion fueled by pressurized Hydrogen Gas Canisters. Double-tap Spacebar to ignite thrusters with blue soul flame exhaust.

### 3. Quantum Ion Repulsor Module (`enchantedwood:ion_repulsor_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Chestplate (Tab 1).
* **Function**: 100% electric vertical flight and hover capability. Draws **25 FE/t** directly from the chestplate's battery buffer without requiring consumable fuel canisters. Negates 100% of fall damage.

### 4. Nanite Repair Matrix (`enchantedwood:nanite_repair_matrix`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Any Piece (Tabs 0–3).
* **Global Suit Network**: Installing the matrix in *any single piece* (such as the Chestplate or Helmet) circulates nanites to auto-repair **all four equipped modular armor pieces simultaneously**, leaving your boots free for Step-Assist and High-Jump!
* **Live Combat Overclocking (v2.1)**: Installed logic chips now dynamically reduce the in-combat auto-repair delay:
  * *No Chip*: 10 seconds combat delay (3 durability / 2s)
  * *Basic Chip*: 7 seconds combat delay (3 durability / 2s)
  * *Advanced Chip*: 4 seconds combat delay (6 durability / 2s)
  * *Quantum Chip*: **0 seconds combat delay** — **instant live combat nanite auto-repair** (15 durability / 2s)!

### 5. Speed Servo Leg Module (`enchantedwood:speed_servo_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Leggings (Tab 2).
* **Function**: Grants continuous **Speed II** while running or sprinting. Emits subtle electric sparks at the player's feet. Drains a modest 2 FE/t only while in motion.

### 6. Hydraulic Step-Assist Boot Module (`enchantedwood:step_assist_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Boots (Tab 3).
* **Function**: Extends step height by **+0.6 blocks**, allowing the player to walk up full 1-block cliffs, stairs, and ledges effortlessly without jumping. Unequipping cleanly resets step height to vanilla defaults.

### 7. High-Jump Actuator Boot Module (`enchantedwood:high_jump_module`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Boots (Tab 3).
* **Function**: Grants **Jump Boost II** (clearing 2.5-block walls easily) and provides **100% fall damage negation** from any height. Consumes 1 FE/t only while airborne.

### 8. Fluoropolymer Acid-Proof Plating (`enchantedwood:acid_proof_plating`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Chestplate (Tab 1) or Leggings (Tab 2).
* **Function**: Grants **Acid Protection**—100% immunity to caustic acid rain and pools in The Convergence. Continuously neutralizes Poison, Wither, and Nausea debuffs. Drains **4 FE/t (~80 FE/s)** only while actively neutralizing caustic hazards (0 FE when idle).

### 9. Thermal Refractory Plating (`enchantedwood:thermal_refractory_plating`)
* **Compatible Slot**: Chestplate (Tab 1) or Leggings (Tab 2).
* **Function**: Grants **Fire Resistance**, rapidly extinguishes fire, and provides swift buoyant swimming in lava. Drains **5 FE/t (~100 FE/s)** only while submerged in fire, lava, or volcanic caldera zones.

## 👑 Infinite Quad-Core God-Mode Synergy

By defeating the Tier 3 boss **The Primordial Cataclysm** 4 times to harvest 4 **Infinite Dimensional Matrices**, players can insert one Matrix into Slot 0 of all 4 exosuit pieces (Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots).

* **Infinite Energy**: All four pieces maintain 100% energy capacity perpetually.
* **Perpetual Flight**: The Quantum Ion Repulsor can fly indefinitely with zero battery drain.
* **Live Nanite Immortality**: Combined with a Quantum Chip and Nanite Repair Matrix, the exosuit regenerates durability live under fire without ever running out of power.
* **Total Environmental Immunity**: Acid Plating and Thermal Plating run without ever exhausting portable battery packs.

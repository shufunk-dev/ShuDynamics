## 🌋 Advanced Superalloys (Alloy Foundry)

Forged inside the **Alloy Foundry** or synthesized in the **Dual-Powered Induction Smelter**:

### 1. Tan-Ti Superalloy Ingot (`enchantedwood:tan_ti_ingot`)
* **Composition**: Tantalum Ingot + Titanium Ingot.
* **Properties**: Extreme biocompatibility and corrosion resistance. Essential for precision pneumatic Hypospray valves, medical needles, and biochemical injectors.

### 2. Hafnium-Tungsten Carbide Ingot (`enchantedwood:hafnium_tungsten_carbide_ingot`)
* **Composition**: Hafnium Ingot + Tungsten Ingot.
* **Properties**: Ultra-high melting point exceeding 4,000°C. Utilized in thermal refractory plating, blast containment, and advanced rocket nozzles.

### 3. Neo-Titanium Spring-Steel Ingot (`enchantedwood:neo_titanium_ingot`)
* **Composition**: Neodymium Magnet + Titanium Ingot.
* **Properties**: Exceptional tensile elasticity and magnetic recoil. Key component in Exosuit High-Jump Actuator Boots and heavy ATV shock suspension systems.

### 4. Reinforced Aerogel Glass (`enchantedwood:aerogel_glass`)
* **Composition**: 4× Aerogel Shards + 4× Glass + 1× Titanium Ingot.
* **Properties**: Ultra-lightweight, hermetically airtight, blast-resistant glass. Required for crafting the **Pressurized ATV Hazard Canopy** and airtight Cleanroom observation windows.

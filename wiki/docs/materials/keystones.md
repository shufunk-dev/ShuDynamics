# 🔮 Dimensional Keystones & Anomaly Anchors

Ancient dimensional disturbances across reality have manifested as **Anomaly Keystones**. When discovered and constructed in high-tech laboratories, these anchors project spatial energy fields that enhance machines, metallurgy, atmospheric protection, and physical stability.

---

## 🏛️ The Six Anomaly Keystones

Across reality, **6 Anomaly Keystones** form the cosmic stabilization matrix. Keystones **1 through 5** are fully discoverable and constructible in **v1.4.0**, with the **6th and final Keystone** arriving in **v1.5.0** to unlock the final dimensional portal!

## ⚠️ Anchor Harvesting Warning
When breaking any Anomaly Keystone in survival mode, **an Enchanted Pickaxe is strictly required**. Breaking an anchor with standard iron, diamond, or non-enchanted tools destabilizes the keystone's spatial matrix and permanently shatters it!

## 💖 Enchanted Health Lockets (Permanent Max HP)

Enchanted Hearts permanently expand your maximum health pool when consumed, granting additional heart containers:

| Item | Health Bonus | Max Extra Hearts | Rarity / Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enchanted Heart** | +2.0 HP (+1 Heart) | +5 Hearts | Infused Heartwood + Enchanted Dust |
| **Iron Enchanted Heart** | +6.0 HP (+3 Hearts) | +10 Hearts | Iron tier upgrade |
| **Gold Enchanted Heart** | +10.0 HP (+5 Hearts) | +15 Hearts | Gold & Magic infusion |
| **Diamond Enchanted Heart** | +14.0 HP (+7 Hearts) | +20 Hearts | Diamond tier infusion |
| **Netherite Enchanted Heart** | +20.0 HP (+10 Hearts) | +30 Hearts (Fireproof) | Netherite & Endgame catalyst |

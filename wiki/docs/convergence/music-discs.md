## 📻 How to Acquire & Play Discs

In-game, music discs are manufactured using the [Harmonic Record Press](/machines/harmonic-record-press). By combining a **Blank Vinyl Disc** with a thematic **Song Catalyst** and 5,000 FE of thermal energy, players can press all 10 collector discs.

Discs can be played in:
* **Vanilla Jukeboxes**: Insert any disc for dimensional auditory immersion.
* **Modular ATV Stereo System**: Slotted into an ATV audio bay to blast soundtracks while cruising across asphalt highways.
* **Cleanroom Intercom Systems**: Stream relaxing ambient audio across industrial facilities.

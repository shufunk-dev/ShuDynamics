## 🛡️ Arena Tether & Retreat Protocol (Anti-Cheese)

All boss gauntlet tiers are bound to their summoning sanctum by dimensional anchors:
* **64-Block Arena Leash (40-Block Vertical)**: If all engaged players retreat further than **64 blocks** horizontally or **40 blocks** vertically away from the Altar, the boss disengages from combat.
* **Teleport & Full Health Reset**: The boss instantly teleports back to the altar dais, displays an alert (*"✦ The Colossus has disengaged and returned to its altar to regenerate! ✦"*), and rapidly regenerates health back to full.
* **Infinite Replayability**: Once slain, the Altar returns to an inactive state and can be re-awakened indefinitely with additional summoning catalysts.

---

## 🏆 Smart Knockout Relic Loot System

To eliminate grind fatigue and ensure fair progression, slaying the Colossus employs a **Smart Knockout System**:
* **1st Defeat**: Guaranteed drop of **1st unobtained Dimensional Relic**.
* **2nd Defeat**: Guaranteed drop of a **2nd distinct unobtained Relic**.
* **3rd Defeat**: Guaranteed drop of the **3rd and final unobtained Relic**.
* **Subsequent Kills**: Drops bonus Fire Crystals, Sulfur Dust, Wasabi Root, Starfruit, and Dragon Fruit on every kill.

## 🗡️ The Dimensional Relics

### 1. Resonance Cleaver (`enchantedwood:resonance_cleaver`)
* **Weapon Class**: Dimensional Greatblade (14 Attack Damage, 1.1 Attack Speed).
* **Special Ability**: Sweeping attacks unleash dimensional sonic shockwaves that pierce armor and hit all adjacent enemies within a 4-block arc.

### 2. Singularity Staff (`enchantedwood:singularity_staff`)
* **Weapon Class**: Gravity Artifice.
* **Special Ability**: Right-click launches a miniature gravity vortex projectile. Upon impact, it pulls all nearby entities within 8 blocks inward and detonates for **12.0 magic damage**.

### 3. Eternal Bento Box (`enchantedwood:eternal_bento_box`) & Omega Bento Box (`enchantedwood:omega_bento_box`)
* **Item Class**: Infinite Multi-Course Artifact Food.
* **Multi-Course System**: Shift + Right-Click to cycle between 3 powerful culinary courses with distinct audio cues and action bar notifications:
  1. **Course 1: Master Feast** — Restores full hunger and saturation, granting Regeneration II and Saturation.
  2. **Course 2: Wasabi Combat Rush** — Grants Strength II, Speed II, and active debuff immunity against Weakness, Mining Fatigue, and Slowness!
  3. **Course 3: Honey Mochi Fortification** — Grants Absorption IV, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, and 100% knockback negation (Kinetic Dampening).
* *Read the full guide in [Convergence Botanicals & Cuisine](/convergence/botanicals-and-cuisine).*

### 4. Infinite Dimensional Matrix (`enchantedwood:infinite_dimensional_matrix`)
* **Item Class**: Apex Perpetual Energy Core.
* **Obtained From**: Slaying the Tier 3 boss, **The Primordial Cataclysm**.
* **Base Grid Power Provider**: When placed into any battery discharge slot (such as inside a Copper, Aluminum, or Steel Battery block), the matrix functions as an inexhaustible, self-sustaining power generator outputting continuous **100,000 FE/t** directly into your energy grid without fuel or maintenance!
* **Exosuit Quad-Core God-Mode**: Slaying the Primordial Cataclysm 4 times to earn 4 Matrices allows players to install one Infinite Dimensional Matrix into each of the 4 [Modular Power Exosuit](/tools-and-armor/modular-exosuit) battery slots. This provides true God-mode: infinite FE, perpetual flight, continuous nanite auto-repair, and immunity to all hazards with zero energy depletion!

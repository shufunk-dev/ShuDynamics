# 🌲 The 7 Lost Biomes Guide

In **ShuDynamics 2.2.0: The Lost Biomes Convergence Overhaul**, catastrophic dimensional ruptures have drawn long-forgotten eras of classic Minecraft geography across space-time directly into **The Convergence** (`enchantedwood:convergence`). 

Seven historic and nostalgic biomes—dating back from classic Alpha terrain to beloved removed biome variants—now seamlessly interlock alongside The Convergence's alien hazard landscapes, featuring full subterranean ore generation, dedicated maritime ocean buffers, and rich flora and fauna.

## 🌿 Detailed Biome Breakdown

### 1. 🌴 Alpha Rainforest (`enchantedwood:alpha_rainforest`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#8cb6ff`, Fog `#c0d8ff`, Water `#247cd5`.
* **Signature Appearance**: Radiates the iconic, electric-green Alpha grass and leaf coloration (`#48b518`) characteristic of Minecraft Alpha 1.1.2_01.
* **Canopy & Undergrowth**: Thick straight Alpha oak trees with dense blob foliage, dripping vines, expansive plains flower beds, and Convergence vegetation.
* **Subterranean**: Full standard ore generation, underground lava springs, monster rooms, and ShuDynamics **Tin Ore**.
* **Fauna & Threats**: Peaceful farm animals roam the canopy floor, but nightfall spawns deadly Convergence Zombies, Skeletons, Creepers, and Spiders.

### 2. 🍂 Seasonal Forest (`enchantedwood:seasonal_forest`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#7ba4ff`, Fog `#bdd4ff`, Water `#2b78d8`.
* **Signature Appearance**: A picturesque autumn landscape. Canopy foliage glows with a fiery autumn amber hue (`#d66b18`), contrasting against rich golden-meadow grass (`#a69a34`).
* **Vegetation & Features**: Densely populated with birch and oak leaf litter undergrowth, wild pumpkin patches, and flower forest flora.
* **Exploration Tip**: Excellent source of wood, decorative foliage, and early food cultivation while exploring The Convergence.

### 3. 🌾 Shrubland (`enchantedwood:shrubland`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#87b5ff`, Fog `#c8dcff`, Water `#326bc7`.
* **Signature Appearance**: An open, rolling transitional savanna scrubland styled after the classic Beta biome, characterized by olive-ochre grass (`#a5b24e`) and foliage (`#919b3e`).
* **Custom Vegetation**: Populated with custom **Shrubland Bushes** (`enchantedwood:shrubland_bush`), scattered dead bushes, and double-tall grass.
* **Fauna**: Ideal terrain for horse breeding and livestock gathering (sheep, pigs, cows, horses).
* **ATV Mobility**: The flat, open terrain makes Shrubland an outstanding biome for testing high-speed ATV runs and laying automated viaducts with the **Road Paver Mk2**.

### 4. 🦜 Modified Jungle Edge (`enchantedwood:modified_jungle_edge`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#77a9ff`, Fog `#bfdbff`, Water `#3f76e4`.
* **Signature Appearance**: A resurrected tribute to one of the rarest biomes in Minecraft history. Features steep, dramatic hillside ridges draped in bright tropical emerald foliage (`#30bb0b`) and grass (`#59c93c`).
* **Flora & Harvests**: Sparse jungle trees, thick bamboo groves, wild melon patches, warm flowers, and hanging vines.
* **Wildlife**: Home to wild **Parrots** and **Ocelots**, alongside chickens and sheep.

### 5. ❄️ Alpha Tundra (`enchantedwood:alpha_tundra`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#c0d8ff`, Fog `#deebff`, Water `#39568c`.
* **Signature Appearance**: A retro winter wonderland featuring pale, icy sage grass (`#80b497`) and subdued foliage (`#60a47e`).
* **Freezing Dynamics**: Actively freezes open water into ice and coats exposed surfaces with top-layer snow. Dotted with classic snowy spruce trees and winter fauna.
* **Wildlife**: Arctic **Polar Bears** and **Rabbits**.
* **Survival Note**: Freezing temperatures can impact suit batteries if unshielded; wear your **Modular Power Exosuit** with thermal refractory plating for comfortable extended surveying.

### 6. 🏜️ Desert Lakes (`enchantedwood:desert_lakes`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#6eb1ff`, Fog `#d2e3ff`, Water `#326bc7`.
* **Signature Appearance**: A lush oasis ecosystem embedded within the arid Convergence wastes. Features olive-toned desert grass (`#bfb755`) and foliage (`#aea444`).
* **Connected Lake Networks**: Tuned multi-noise parameters spawn up to **4 natural spring-fed oasis lakes per chunk**, fringed with golden sand rims, dense sugar cane, and cacti.
* **Structures & Wildlife**: Roaming **Camel** herds, rabbits, and historic **Desert Wells**.
* **Hydration Advantage**: Provides essential fresh water for hydrating nearby volcanic soil and rice paddies without exposure to the Caustic Mire's toxic acid waters.

### 7. ⛰️ Gravelly Mountains (`enchantedwood:gravelly_mountains`)
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Sky `#7daaff`, Fog `#c4dbff`, Water `#3f76e4`.
* **Signature Appearance**: Soaring, jagged windswept alpine peaks carpeted in expansive disks of exposed gravel, stony outcroppings, and slate-green grass (`#8ab689`).
* **Flora & Trees**: High-altitude windswept hills pine trees and alpine shrubbery.
* **Wildlife**: Agile **Mountain Goats** and woolly **Llamas**.
* **Mining & Excavation**: The sheer gravel cliffs are prime locations for testing **3×3 Heavy Mining Hammers** and ATV front-mounted **Mining Drill Bits**.

## 🌊 Dedicated Oceanic Transitions & Beaches

Previous versions of dimensional rifts suffered from unnatural cliff drop-offs into the void. In **v2.2.0**, The Convergence incorporates dedicated multi-noise ocean generation:

* **Warm Ocean (`minecraft:warm_ocean`)**: Tropical marine channels connecting coastal rainforests and jungle edges.
* **Standard Ocean (`minecraft:ocean`)**: Vast temperate sea lanes enabling boat navigation and naval ATV amphibious transit.
* **Frozen Ocean (`minecraft:frozen_ocean`)**: Frigid polar waters fringing the Alpha Tundra with packed ice formations.
* **Sandy Beaches (`minecraft:beach`)**: Natural low-slope sandy buffers providing seamless transitions between landmasses and oceanic basins.

## ⛏️ Subterranean Resources & Ore Generation

Every Lost Biome features comprehensive subterranean mineral generation matching standard Overworld distributions:

* **Vanilla Minerals**: Dirt, gravel, granite, diorite, andesite, and tuff pockets.
* **Coal Ore**: Upper and lower clusters throughout mountain ridges and deep caves.
* **Iron Ore**: Distributed across middle, upper, and small high-density veins.
* **Precious Ores**: Gold, Redstone, Diamond (buried and deep veins), Lapis Lazuli, and Copper.
* **ShuDynamics Tin Ore**: Subterranean deposits of **Tin Ore** (`enchantedwood:tin_ore`) spawn abundantly across all 7 Lost Biomes, enabling early-to-midgame bronze metallurgy without returning to the Overworld.

## ⚠️ Hazards & Mutant Mob Spawns

While the Lost Biomes offer breathable air and safe, non-acidic rainfall compared to the hostile **Caustic Mire**, **Scorched Caldera**, or **Anoxic Barrens**, dimensional instability remains high:

1. **Mutant Hostiles**: All standard monsters that spawn at night or in caves are converted into lethal dimensional mutants:
   * **Convergence Zombies**: High speed and damage, dropping **Sulfur Dust**.
   * **Convergence Skeletons**: Rapid charged resonance arrows with tactical teleport blinking.
   * **Convergence Creepers**: Gravitational suction during hissing with lingering acid fallout clouds.
   * **Convergence Spiders**: Long-range acid web shooting.
2. **Transitioning to Alien Biomes**: When driving or flying from a Lost Biome into adjacent alien core biomes (such as entering the Scorched Caldera or descending into anoxic caverns), environmental hazard alerts will trigger. Ensure your **Modular Power Helmet**, **Hypospray Cartridges**, or **Pressurized ATV Canopy** are active before crossing biome thresholds!

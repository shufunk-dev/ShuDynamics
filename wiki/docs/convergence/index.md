# 🌌 The Convergence Dimension & Hazards Guide

**The Convergence** (`enchantedwood:convergence`) is an unstable, fractured dimension introduced in **ShuDynamics 2.0** and overhauled with 7 historic biomes in **v2.2.0: The Lost Biomes Overhaul**. Piercing through dimensional reality, it features lethal alien hazards, nostalgic restored landmasses, hostile mutant mob conversions, caustic acid waters, extreme volcanic calderas, and atmospheric hypoxia.

---

## 🌀 Reaching The Convergence: Constructing the Gateway

The Gateway to The Convergence cannot be found naturally—you must construct it yourself using **Crying Obsidian** and all **6 Dimensional Anomaly Keystones**:

### 1. The 6 Required Keystone Blocks
Across your progression, you must craft and assemble the six cosmic anchor blocks:
1. 🌬️ **Atmospheric Anchor** (`enchantedwood:atmospheric_anchor`)
2. ⚡ **Kinetic Anchor** (`enchantedwood:kinetic_anchor`)
3. 🔥 **Thermal Anchor** (`enchantedwood:thermal_anchor`)
4. 🛠️ **Metallurgical Anchor** (`enchantedwood:metallurgical_anchor`)
5. ⚛️ **Plasma Anchor** (`enchantedwood:plasma_anchor`)
6. 🌌 **Dimensional Singularity Anchor** (`enchantedwood:dimensional_singularity`)

### 2. Portal Frame Architecture
* **Frame Dimensions**: Construct a vertical rectangular frame identical in dimensions to a standard Nether Portal, featuring an interior opening of **2 blocks wide by 3 blocks high** (overall frame footprint of 4×5 blocks; corner blocks are optional).
* **Frame Composition**: The 10 non-corner perimeter frame blocks must contain:
  * **All 6 distinct Anomaly Keystones** placed in any non-corner frame position.
  * **At least 4 Crying Obsidian** blocks filling the remaining non-corner border slots.

### 3. Portal Ignition & Breach
* Once the frame is complete, **right-click any block of the frame** (or place the final **Dimensional Singularity** into the frame).
* If all 6 Keystones and 4 Crying Obsidian are present, the keystones achieve critical harmonic resonance:
  * An announcement alerts all players: *"✦ [Spatial Sensors] The 6 Keystones achieve critical harmonic resonance! The dimensional barrier ruptures—the Gateway to The Convergence is OPEN!"*
  * End rod and reverse-portal particles erupt, and the interior fills with swirling **Dormant Rift** dimensional energy.
* Step into the rift to cross the dimensional threshold into **The Convergence**!
* *Caution*: Ensure adequate life-support and environmental protections (such as a Modular Power Exosuit, Hypospray Cartridges, or protective gourmet rolls) are equipped before stepping through.

## 🗺️ Dimensional Biomes

The Convergence is divided into distinct regions: **4 Alien Core Hazard Biomes**, **7 Nostalgic Lost Biomes (v2.2)**, and dedicated **Oceanic Maritime Transitions**:

### ☣️ Alien Core Biomes

#### 1. Caustic Mire
* **Environment**: Toxic wetlands and acid pools overgrown with bioluminescent fungi.
* **Flora & Minerals**: Home to **Wild Wasabi** along riverbanks, subterranean **Fluorite Crystal Ore**, and deep **Tantalum Ore**.
* **Hazard**: Severe Caustic Acid Rain and pools. Standing in water or rain without acid protection causes continuous damage.

#### 2. Scorched Caldera
* **Environment**: High-temperature volcanic basalt fields and active magma flows.
* **Flora & Minerals**: Subterranean **Zirconia (Baddeleyite) Ore** along volcanic walls, **Wild Dragon Fruit** growing in volcanic ash, and deep **Hafnium Ore**.
* **Hazard**: Extreme Caldera Hyperthermia. Ambient convective heat will ignite and burn players lacking thermal shielding.

#### 3. Anoxic Barrens
* **Environment**: Desolate, airless crater plateaus shrouded in perpetual void rifts.
* **Flora & Minerals**: Deep deposits of **Neodymium Ore** and rare **Silica Aerogel Ore**.
* **Hazard**: Complete zero-oxygen atmosphere at all elevations. Suffocation occurs rapidly without active life support.

#### 4. Riftwood Haven
* **Environment**: Dense dimensional forests featuring celestial tree species.
* **Flora & Minerals**: Giant **Starfruit Trees** and **Avocado Trees** yielding woodsets, saplings, and exotic fruits. Subterranean Fluorite and Tantalum deposits.

### 🌲 The 7 Lost Biomes (v2.2.0 Overhaul)

Dimensional rifts have drawn forgotten terrain from classic and removed eras of Minecraft into The Convergence:

* 🌴 **Alpha Rainforest**: Vibrant electric green foliage (`#48b518`), Alpha oak canopies, plains flora, and vines.
* 🍂 **Seasonal Forest**: Fiery autumn amber canopies (`#d66b18`), golden meadow grass (`#a69a34`), leaf litter, and pumpkins.
* 🌾 **Shrubland**: Classic rolling scrubland, custom shrubland bushes (`shrubland_bush`), dead bushes, and horses.
* 🦜 **Modified Jungle Edge**: Nostalgic steep ridges, emerald foliage (`#30bb0b`), melons, bamboo, parrots, and ocelots.
* ❄️ **Alpha Tundra**: Retro sub-zero winter wonderland with sage grass (`#80b497`), snowy trees, freezing surface ice, and polar bears.
* 🏜️ **Desert Lakes**: Verdant desert oasis basin with connected spring lake clusters (4 per chunk), sand rims, sugar cane, and camels.
* ⛰️ **Gravelly Mountains**: Rugged windswept alpine slopes, expansive gravel disks, pine forests, and mountain goats.

## 🧟 Hostile Mob Variants

Natural hostile spawns inside The Convergence are mutated into dangerous dimensional variants:

### 1. Convergence Zombie
* **Appearance**: Void-darkened skin reinforced with jagged crystalline armor plates.
* **Abilities**: High movement speed, increased attack damage, drops **Sulfur Dust** upon death.

### 2. Convergence Skeleton
* **Appearance**: Resonant crystalline ribcage humming with dimensional energy.
* **Abilities**: Fires high-velocity charged resonance arrows that inflict vulnerability. Utilizes **Tactical Blink** to teleport away when closed into melee range.

### 3. Convergence Creeper
* **Appearance**: Obsidian void body cracked with luminous glowing fissures.
* **Abilities**: Emits an ominous gravitational pull when hissing, drawing nearby players inward. Leaves a lingering corrosive acid cloud upon detonation that damages armor and inflicts Poison II.

### 4. Convergence Spider
* **Appearance**: Caustic arachnid dripping glowing digestive enzymes.
* **Abilities**: Ranged acidic web-shooter that slows and poisons targets from afar.

### 🕳️ Continuous Subterranean Caves & Rift Chasms

* **3D Cave Systems**: All 12 Convergence biomes utilize `minecraft:cave_extra_underground` carvers, seamlessly linking surface cave mouths down into deepslate caverns, dripstone pockets, and mineshaft networks.
* **Overhauled Rift Chasms**:
  * **Landscape Balance**: Occurrence frequency adjusted so chasms serve as rare, dramatic spelunking gateways rather than repetitive terrain clutter.
  * **Seamless Rim Blending**: Surface stone scarring eliminated; natural grass, floral covers, and sand run cleanly to the chasm lip.
  * **Deep Descent & Branch Tunnels**: Chasms plunge straight down into deepslate ($Y = -15$ to $-40$) and spawn **3 to 4 winding horizontal branch tunnels (35–60 blocks long)** radiating into the surrounding rock, directly breaching into natural caves, mineshafts, and deep dark ruins.

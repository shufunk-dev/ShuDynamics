## 🎮 Controls & Modularity Dashboard

- **Mount / Ride**: Right-Click the ATV with an empty hand.
- **Drive**: Use standard **W** (Accelerate), **S** (Brake / Reverse), **A** (Steer Left), **D** (Steer Right).
- **Open Dashboard**: Right-Click the ATV with an empty hand while mounted or stationary, or press your interact key.
- **Retrieve Vehicle**: **Shift + Right-Click** the vehicle with an empty hand or Wrench to pick up the ATV item, preserving all installed modules, fuel levels, and trunk cargo!

---

## 🏭 Vehicle Fabricator & Tuning Bay

The **Vehicle Fabricator & Tuning Bay** is the dedicated automotive workshop workstation for assembling and customizing modular ATVs:

1. **Assembly / Fabrication**: Place your chosen **Seat**, **Engine**, **Chassis**, **Suspension**, **Tires (4)**, and optional **Cargo Trunk** into the blueprint grid, then click the **[ 🛠️ ]** button to synthesize your custom ATV.
2. **Hot-Swap Modification**: Place any existing ATV into the top modification slot. The fabricator automatically unpacks the installed modules onto the workbench. Swap in upgraded engines, studded tires, or larger trunks, and click **[ 🛠️ ]** to apply upgrades!
3. **Power Requirement**: Consumes **100 FE** per fabrication or modification operation. Connect power cables or insert an Energy Battery.

## 🛠️ Modular Component Tiers

### 1. Engine Tiers
| Engine Tier | Material | Top Speed | Acceleration | Fuel Efficiency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Copper Starter Engine** | Copper + Piston | ~25 km/h | Normal | Standard |
| **Aluminum Agile V4 Engine** | Aluminum + Copper Engine | ~40 km/h | Fast | High |
| **Steel High-Torque V8 Engine**| Steel + Aluminum Engine | ~55 km/h | Very Fast | Heavy-Duty |
| **Titanium Twin-Turbo Engine** | Titanium + Netherite Ingot | ~80 km/h | Nitro-Charged | High-Octane Optimized |

### 2. Tire & Traction Tiers
- **Rubber Off-Road Tire**: Standard all-terrain rubber grip.
- **Steel-Rim Heavy Tire**: Reinforced rim durability for rocky terrain.
- **Titanium-Studded Ice Tire**: Extreme traction on ice, packed ice, snow, and steep slopes.

### 3. Suspension & Chassis Upgrades
- **Steel Suspension**: 1.0 block step-up height with standard shock dampening.
- **Titanium Heavy-Duty Suspension**: **1.5 block auto-step-up height** allowing you to drive up ledges without jumping!
- **Chassis Frames (Aluminum, Steel, Titanium)**: Dictates maximum vehicle durability and future hazard shielding in anomaly dimensions.

### 4. Cargo Trunks
- **Small Cargo Trunk**: 9-slot internal rack storage.
- **Medium Cargo Trunk**: 18-slot expanded storage.
- **Large Cargo Trunk**: 27-slot full chest mobile capacity.

# ✨ ShuDynamics — Tech, Magic, Vehicles & Power Progression

**ShuDynamics** is an all-in-one industrial technology, modular vehicles, magic, tiered energy, and digital storage progression mod for **Minecraft Fabric 1.21.11+**. 

Designed for seamless survival progression—from early-game wooden enchantments and metallurgy to high-voltage electrical grids, modular all-terrain exploration vehicles, automated highway construction, deep-sea life support, and infinite wireless crystal storage networks.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🏎️ 1. Modular All-Terrain Vehicles (ATV) & Tuning Bay *(v1.3 New!)*
* **Drivable All-Terrain Vehicles (ATV):** High-speed, responsive exploration vehicle built to traverse rugged mountains, caves, and paved highway networks.
* **6 Swappable Module Slots:** Tune your vehicle in the **Vehicle Fabricator**:
  * **Engines:** Copper, Aluminum, Steel, and Titanium powerplants.
  * **Tires:** Rubber Off-Road, Steel-Rim Heavy, and Titanium Studded.
  * **Suspensions & Shocks:** High-tension springs granting **100% cliff fall damage immunity**.
  * **Chassis Armor:** Aluminum, Steel, and Titanium protective plating.
  * **Cargo Trunks:** Integrated Small, Medium, and Large mobile inventory storage beds.
  * **Hybrid Power:** Runs on Biofuel, Gasoline, High-Octane Racing Fuel, or charges directly from power cables!
* **Item Retrieval:** Shift + Right-Click to pick up the vehicle item with all installed components, fuel, and trunk cargo preserved.

---

### 🛣️ 2. Automated Road Infrastructure & Highways *(v1.3 New!)*
* **Asphalt Roads & Slabs:** Paved road surfaces providing a continuous movement speed boost on foot and unlocking a **+25% top speed multiplier** for ATVs.
* **Auto-Connecting Concrete Curbs:** Multi-directional curbs with automatic corner connections (`Straight`, `Inner`, and `Outer` bends) and 20 custom collision shapes.
* **Road Transition Ramps:** Dual-mode ramps (`Ground` and `Road` modes) for seamless vehicle on/off-ramps.
* **Autonomous Road Paver:** Heavy-duty machine that consumes energy/fuel to automatically lay 3-wide asphalt roads and extrude concrete curbs on both sides.

---

### 🛢️ 3. Petrochemicals, Biofuels & Refining *(v1.3 New!)*
* **Sweet Corn Agriculture:** Fast-growing 8-stage crop providing high-saturation food and bio-ethanol feedstock.
* **Oil Sand Worldgen:** Naturally generating deposits in arid biomes yielding **Crude Oil Sludge**.
* **The Fuel Refinery:** Powered distillation machine converting crude oil and agricultural crops into **Gasoline Canisters**, **Biofuel Canisters**, **High-Octane Racing Fuel**, and **Mineral Tar**.

---

### ⚡ 4. 3-Tier Energy & Power Grids
* **Tiered Clean Energy:** Generate FE with Copper, Aluminum, and Steel Generators.
* **High-Capacity Batteries:** Large internal energy buffers to power heavy industrial machinery.
* **Low-Loss Cable Networks:** Directional insulated cables for seamless power distribution.
* **Smart Hybrid Failover:** Machines automatically switch to emergency solid fuel if energy reserves run dry.

---

### 📦 5. Digital Crystal Storage Networks
* **Enchanted Storage Controller:** Central mainframe featuring ambient positional audio, dual hybrid power inputs, and 3 expansion bays.
* **6-Slot Drive Bay:** Accommodates 1k–64k Crystal Storage Drives with dynamic **Green $\rightarrow$ Yellow $\rightarrow$ Purple $\rightarrow$ Red** LED capacity matrix and anti-data loss extraction locks.
* **54-Slot Digital Storage Terminal:** Unified 6x9 network interface with instant item search and retrieval.
* **Chunk Loader & Interdimensional Cards:** Keep your storage network active across the entire Overworld or access items across dimensions (Nether & The End).

---

### 🌌 6. Dimensional Keystones & The Keystone Nexus
* **Atmospheric Anchor (Keystone #1):** Master deep-sea oceanic pressure in full Scuba Gear to unlock.
* **Kinetic Anchor (Keystone #2 - v1.3 New!):** Master terrestrial mobility by assembling and driving an ATV.
* **The Keystone Nexus:** 7-node progression tracking the discovery of all dimensional anomalies.

### 🤿 7. Diving Equipment & Deep-Sea Life Support
* **Snorkel:** Surface-breathing oxygen replenishment and extended diving lung capacity.
* **Modular Scuba Suit:**
  * **Diving Mask:** Conduit Vision and rapid underwater mining.
  * **Scuba Tank Chestplate:** Infinite pressurized oxygen supply.
  * **Wetsuit Leggings:** Streamlined polymer granting Dolphin’s Grace swimming.
  * **Diving Flippers:** High-speed underwater propulsion.

---

### 🛡️ 7. Tiered Tools, Armor & 3x3 Mining Hammers
* **Tier Progression:** Tin, Bronze, Aluminum, Steel, Titanium, Enchanted Diamond, and Enchanted Netherite gear.
* **3x3 Mining Hammers:** Heavy-duty excavation tools designed for rapid subterranean mining.
* **Special Archetypes:** Livingwood weapons and Barkskin equipment offering unique passives.
* **Sanctuary Wards:** Enchanted Lamps with a 32-block hostile mob spawn prevention aura.

## 📥 Installation & Requirements

1. Make sure you have **Minecraft 1.21.11** installed.
2. Install **Fabric Loader** (`0.19.3` or newer).
3. Download and place **Fabric API** into your `.minecraft/mods` directory.
4. Place **`shudynamics-1.3.0.jar`** into your `mods` folder.
5. Launch the game and enjoy!

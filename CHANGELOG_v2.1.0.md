# ShuDynamics v2.1.0 — The Harmonic Convergence Update 🎶⚡

Welcome to **ShuDynamics v2.1.0**! This major release introduces our complete 10-track original soundtrack with survival pressing mechanics, major combat quality-of-life protections for Modular Power Suits, multi-course culinary relics, and boundless creative-tier energy infrastructure.

---

### 🎵 10-Track Original Soundtrack & Harmonic Record Press
* **10 Original Music Discs**: Authentic, high-fidelity streamed tracks composed specifically for ShuDynamics and fully playable in any vanilla Jukebox:
  1. **ShuDynamics - Rip the Sky Wide** *(The Convergence Main Theme)*
  2. **ShuDynamics - Rift of the Colossus** *(Boss Battle Theme)*
  3. **ShuDynamics - Highway Overdrive** *(Roadways & High-Speed Driving)*
  4. **ShuDynamics - Sterile Protocol** *(Cleanroom Complex & Laboratories)*
  5. **ShuDynamics - Subroutine 64k** *(Digital Storage Networks & Automation)*
  6. **ShuDynamics - Haven Bloom (The Bento Groove)** *(Cuisine & Relaxation)*
  7. **ShuDynamics - Heart of the Crucible** *(Nether Metallurgy & Extreme Heat)*
  8. **ShuDynamics - Stratosphere Break** *(High-Altitude Jetpack Flight)*
  9. **ShuDynamics - Abyssal Pressure** *(Trench Exploration & Marine Depths)*
  10. **ShuDynamics - Anoxic Echoes** *(Space, Void & Cosmic Dimensions)*
* **Harmonic Record Press**: A brand-new industrial machine used to stamp Blank Vinyl Discs into master music records using thematic ingredients and FE power. Fully integrated with EMI and JEI recipe viewers!
* **Survival Acquisition**: Blank vinyl discs can be crafted with Tar or Polymer Sheets, and rare master copies can also be discovered inside Bento Boxes and boss reward caches.

### 🛡️ Modular Power Suit: Emergency Chassis Lock
* **Zero Item Destruction**: When any piece of your Modular Power Armor takes fatal durability damage in prolonged combat, the chassis locks safely at **1 HP** instead of shattering.
* **Module Preservation**: Installed Quantum Computer Chips, High-Capacity Batteries, and Nanite Auto-Repair Matrices will **never** be lost or destroyed.
* **Combat Nanite Overclock**: Logic Core processors now scale the combat delay for Nanite Auto-Repair:
  * *Unchipped*: 10s combat cooldown
  * *Basic Computer Chip*: 6s combat cooldown
  * *Advanced Computer Chip*: 3s combat cooldown
  * *Quantum Computer Chip*: **0s cooldown** (continuous live repairs under active fire!)
* **Auto-Reboot**: The instant nanites or an anvil restore durability above 1 HP, the suit automatically boots back online with an audible chime and system notice.

---

### 🍱 Multi-Course Bento Boxes (Eternal & Omega)
* **Interactive Menu**: Hold **Shift + Right-Click** to cycle between 3 distinct culinary courses on the fly:
  * **Course 1 (Colossus / Celestial Feast)**: Full hunger, 1-2m Tri-Shield hazard immunity (Acid, Thermal, Atmospheric), Regeneration & Absorption.
  * **Course 2 (Wasabi Combat Rush)**: Instantly strips Weakness, Slowness & debuffs, granting continuous 5-minute debuff immunity (Adrenaline Rush), +25% attack speed, Haste II, and Strength II (Omega). Perfect for countering high-tier boss debuffs!
  * **Course 3 (Honey Mochi Fortification)**: Kinetic Dampening (completely negates fall/kinetic impact damage), Resistance, and heavy Absorption.
* **Accelerated Omega Cooldown**: The Omega Bento Box recharges in only **60 seconds**!

### ⚡ Infinite Dimensional Matrix (Apex Power Core)
* **Pinnacle Relic**: Dropped by defeating **The Primordial Cataclysm (Tier 3)**.
* **Limitless Base Grid Power**: Place into the **Discharge Slot (Slot 0)** of any Copper, Aluminum, Steel, or Tungsten Battery Block to discharge at up to 100,000 FE/t, holding your entire base grid at 100% capacity forever with zero fuel or generators needed.
* **Modular Suit Infinite Battery**: Slot into any Modular Power Armor piece's battery slot for truly limitless FE that never drains. Defeat the Cataclysm 4 times to construct a completely self-powered, infinite God-mode suit!
